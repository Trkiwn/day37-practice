The Process of Making Vietnamese Fish Sauce (Nước Mắm)
1. Fresh anchovies are mixed with salt in large wooden barrels.
2. The mixture is left to ferment for a period of 12 to 18 months.
3. During fermentation, the liquid is periodically stirred and exposed to sunlight.
4. Once fermentation is complete, the liquid is drained and filtered.
5. The filtered sauce is bottled and labeled.
6. The bottled sauce is distributed to markets for sale.
Type:
4.Rewrite each active sentence in correct passive voice.Passive Transformation
a) "Workers pour the hot liquid into moulds."
b) "They heat the mixture to 90 degrees for ten minutes."
c) "Machines cut the sheets into equal strips."

The Process of Sorting Recyclable Waste at a Facility
1. Collected waste arrives at the facility and is placed on a conveyor belt.
2. The waste is sorted into three categories: paper, plastic, and metal.
3a. Paper is compressed into bales.   3b. Plastic is washed and shredded.   3c. Metal is melted down into blocks.
4. All three materials are then transported to separate manufacturing plants for reuse.
3.Write only the final, closing sentence for this cyclical process -- the one that shows the loop.Sentence Writing
